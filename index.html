The Duo

Suddenly Dragged is a collaboration between Sage (@suddenlymadesage) and their uncle John (@dragged2death) — two artists, one lineage, working out loud together.

The persona stays faceless on purpose. This is about the work and the wandering character behind it, not a face to follow.
