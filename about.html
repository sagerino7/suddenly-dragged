About

"same blood, same quest"

Suddenly Dragged is a collaboration between Sage Cameron (@suddenlymadesage) and their uncle John (@dragged2death) — two artists from the same lineage, painting together since 2024.

The name blends their individual identities into one shared one. The work explores color, symbolism, and hip-hop influence, filtered through family and generational lineage — a way for both of them to get experimental and step outside their usual creative paths.

The persona behind it stays faceless, or at least less face-forward. It's about the work and the wandering character running through it — not a personality to follow.

The launch collection pairs three new joint works with one existing piece from each of them, kicking off a steady run of collaborative paintings, mixed media, apparel, and the occasional hidden easter egg for anyone paying close attention.
